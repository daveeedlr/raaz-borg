THE GRAND PRIX PROTOCOL EL PROTOCOLO GRAND PRIX

White Carbon Combat begins with eight qualifiers. Higher-level Grand Prix tournaments reserve seven qualifying spots, with the eighth occupied by the champion promoted from the preceding league. Each Grand Prix awards a cash prize to its winner, who can advance to the next league. White Carbon Combat comienza con ocho clasificados. Los Grand Prix de niveles superiores reservan siete cupos clasificatorios y el octavo corresponde al campeón ascendido de la liga anterior. Cada Grand Prix entrega un premio económico a su ganador, quien puede avanzar a la siguiente liga.
