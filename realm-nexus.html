HOW THE EIGHT SPOTS ARE FILLED CÓMO SE LLENAN LOS OCHO CUPOS

In White Carbon Combat, all eight spots are earned in qualifying. In the higher leagues, seven spots are earned in qualifying and the eighth goes to the champion promoted from the previous league. En White Carbon Combat, los ocho cupos se ganan en las clasificatorias. En las ligas superiores, siete cupos se ganan en esa fase y el octavo le corresponde al campeón que asciende de la liga anterior.
